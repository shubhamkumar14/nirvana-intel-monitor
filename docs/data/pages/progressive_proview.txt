How does it work?
Snapshot ProView utilizes a device that plugs directly into your vehicle's diagnostic port. It tracks your vehicle's speed and location to gain further insight into your drivers' habits. It allows us to not only offer fleet management tools to your business but also provide you with a personalized commercial auto rate when your policy is up for renewal.
Drive more efficiently with free fleet management tools
Businesses that have three or more vehicles enrolled in Snapshot ProView gain access to a free fleet management dashboard, which is a valuable business tool that allows you to better coach your drivers. The tool includes:
Vehicle tracking
Get near-live updates that show you where your vehicles are, any time.
Geofencing
Get notifications any time one of your vehicles leaves a pre-defined area.
Trip reporting
Get summaries of each route completed by your commercial vehicles.
Safety scorecards
Receive safety scores emailed monthly with tips on how to improve your score.
Instantly save at least 5% upon enrollment plus customized rates
Just for enrolling you’ll automatically receive a 5% discount on your Progressive Commercial auto policy premium. New customers save an average of 9% with Snapshot ProView.
The info received from Snapshot ProView also helps us give your business a personalized rate based on your employees' driving habits. Keep an eye on your safety scorecard each month and follow the tips within to improve those habits, and you could save between 8% and 20% at renewal. ††
Is my business eligible?
All commercial auto policies are eligible for enrollment in Snapshot ProView, including current Progressive Commercial auto policyholders. The lone exception is trucks that utilize an Electronic Logging Device (ELD).
Businesses with three or more vehicles enrolled in Snapshot ProView will have access to the full suite of fleet management tools, while businesses with 1-2 vehicles only receive monthly emails with their safety scorecard. All enrolled policies receive the discount.
While truckers and truck driving businesses that use ELDs are not eligible for Snapshot ProView, we offer a similar program for them called Smart Haul .
