Corporate Risk
Build a safer, stronger business from the inside out.
Running a national fleet is complex: shifting regulations, decentralized teams and compliance pressures. Our National Primary Solutions go beyond risk management to solve business challenges. With integrated benefits, insurance and risk strategies we help protect your people, your assets and your trajectory.
Asset Light Fleets
Protect your operations with a purpose-built independent contractor strategy.
Standard coverage may not address the unique needs of businesses that rely on independent contractors. We offer solutions through proprietary tools like OverDrive, automating the majority of onboarding and settlement tasks. With real-time data, compliance support and scalable safety solutions, we help you stay protected, resilient and ready to grow.
Final Mile
Deliver more with less risk.
Final mile fleets face tight timelines, high contractor turnover, limited visibility and thin margins. We help provide coverage designed to address these risks with flexible insurance options, real-time tracking and a streamlined tech stack. Through partners like Haul and our Insurance Exchange, we provide coverage and tools that support safety, help reduce downtime impact and ease administrative tasks — so you stay in control.
Dealers & Lenders
Unlock new revenue at the point of sale.
Dealers and lenders know the frustration of watching customers delay or walk away from deals due to insurance hassles. We simplify the process by embedding coverage options into your sales and financing workflow. Our user-friendly platform offers fast, compliant access to commercial truck insurance, so in many cases customers can leave the lot with coverage in place and you gain a new source of revenue. It's more than a value-add; it's a strategic business tool.
Employee Benefits
Help your people stay healthy and productive.
Retaining talent starts with offering the right benefits. We deliver customized Employee Benefits solutions for transportation workforces, from access to cost-effective health insurance options through TrueChoices to advanced admin support. Discover comprehensive support tools for enrollment, compliance processes and reporting. We also offer personalized rollout communications, HR consulting, access to legal resources and a dedicated service team in Cedar Rapids.
Risk & Workforce Solutions
Proactive risk management.
Our team helps clients manage the total cost of risk by supporting compliance efforts, claims mitigation and safety strategies before incidents occur. By integrating technologies designed to help reduce costs and scalable processes, we empower businesses to optimize operations and stay competitive. This holistic strategy provides peace of mind both on and off the road.
