Last Mile and Final Mile Delivery Insurance Solutions
Why Last Mile and Final Mile Insurance Matters for Delivery Businesses
Every delivery counts. But the last mile and, increasingly, the final mile comes with unique risks that can impact your bottom line. Whether you manage a small courier fleet or oversee large-scale logistics operations, having the right insurance safeguards your drivers, vehicles, and cargo when it matters most.
At Brown & Brown, we specialize in last mile and final mile delivery insurance programs built exclusively for delivery and logistics businesses. Drawing on our deep industry experience, we deliver flexible, scalable coverage programs backed by AM Best A-rated carriers.
Rising Risks in Last Mile and Final Mile Logistics
From package theft to liability accidents, today’s delivery landscape presents more challenges than ever. A single incident — such as a driver auto incident and/or injury, lost cargo, or on-premises accident — can cost thousands. Delivery insurance helps protect your fleet, contractors, and business reputation from these financial risks.
Who Needs Last Mile and Final Mile Delivery Coverage?
Small fleets with both asset and non-asset exposure: Courier owners balancing multiple roles (operations, HR, accounting) who need reliable coverage that meets your business needs and encompasses liabilities from fleet assets as well as exposures derived from independent contractor drivers.
Large fleets and enterprises: CFOs and risk managers focused on cost savings, compliance, and reducing liability exposure wherever possible.
Gig and e-commerce providers: Businesses leveraging 1099 drivers, as well as e-bikes, by utilizing app-based platforms to deliver timely merchandise to the end consumers.
Tailored Insurance Options for Your Delivery Operations
Fleet Auto Liability and Physical Damage
Helps protect owned, leased, and long-term rented vehicles with scalable options — including coverage options for courier vans, delivery trucks as well as tractors. Coverage enhancements can include up to $2M in primary coverage limits with programs offerings encompassing flat rated annual premiums as well as pay-as-you-go mileage/trip usage platforms.
Cargo Loss, Theft, and Warehouse Liability
Covers motor truck cargo, including protection against theft, including first party theft/dishonesty, spoilage, and cyber hijacking from potential cyber related breaches.
General Liability and On-Premises Protection
From crossing the customer’s threshold to assembling goods indoors, this coverage helps your business be protected where accidents can happen.
Hired and Non-owned Auto Liability (HNOA)
Ideal for businesses using rental fleets as well as 1099 subcontractor drivers. With flexible, pay-as-you-go reporting, you only pay for the coverage you need.
Additional Insurance Coverages
Workers’ compensation: Protects your employees from the financial impact of work-related injuries or illnesses. For delivery businesses, workers’ compensation covers medical expenses, lost wages, and rehabilitation.
Occupational accident for 1099 drivers/contingent liability: Provides medical and disability benefits for independent contractors injured on the job. Contingent liability coverage helps protect businesses from potential legal challenges around driver classification.
Errors and omissions (E&O) for specialized deliveries: Covers claims of negligence, mistakes, or delays that may cause financial harm to clients. This helps ensure specialized deliveries — such as medical or high-value cargo — are backed by strong risk protection.
Crime insurance and employee dishonesty: Helps protect against losses from theft, fraud, or dishonest acts committed by employees or contractors. Critical for businesses handling high-value goods and sensitive deliveries.
Umbrella and excess liability towers: Provides higher liability limits above your existing policies, helping ensure protection against catastrophic losses that exceed standard coverage.
While under dispatch auto insurance: Designed for the gig economy, this coverage helps protect independent drivers while actively engaged in a delivery. It covers where personal auto insurance may not apply.

FAQs About Last Mile and Final Mile Delivery Insurance
What is Last Mile Delivery Insurance?
Last mile delivery insurance helps protect businesses during the most critical stage of logistics—the handoff from hub to customer. It helps cover liability, cargo, and driver-related risks.
What Does Last Mile Delivery Insurance Cover?
Last mile insurance typically covers auto liability, cargo, and general liability. Common protections offered include:
Auto liability and physical damage
Motor truck cargo loss and theft
General liability for on-premises incidents
Hired and non-owned auto liability
Occupational accident coverage for 1099 drivers
What Other Risk Management Strategies Can Last Mile Delivery Insurance Include?
Many policies add cost-saving or safety measures beyond basic coverage. These may include telematics discounts, cyber hijacking protection, pay-as-you-go reporting, and fraud prevention programs designed for logistics fleets.
How Much Does Final Mile Insurance Cost?
Final mile delivery insurance costs vary by fleet size, driver type, delivery volume, and coverage limits. Flexible, pay-as-you-go reporting helps businesses control costs.
Can Independent Contractors Get Last Mile and Final Mile Delivery Coverage?
Yes. Independent contractors and 1099 drivers can purchase specialized plans. Coverage often includes occupational accident insurance, contingent liability protection, and under-dispatch auto coverage tailored to gig economy drivers.
